TECHMEN 5669 · How To Use It

Keep this with the driver station

Before every match or practice

  1. Robot OFF. Turret on the HOME mark. Hood pushed all the way down by hand. Intake pushed all the way up. Climber all the way down.
  2. Turn the robot on while holding the hood down.
  3. Driver Station USB tab: driver controller in slot 0, operator in slot 1. Press a button to see which row lights up; drag to fix. F1 = rescan.
  4. Driver Station Team Station: pick our alliance color (Red / Blue). At competition the field sets it.
  5. Elastic: "Pit Check" should say ALL GO.
  6. Practice only (a match sets it): robot straight away from your driver station, hold Driver LB until it buzzes.
  7. Shop field: "Practice Field" switch ON. Competition: OFF.

Emergency keys (Driver Station laptop)

  • Enter = Disable (stops the robot, no restart needed). Use this one.
  • Space = Emergency stop. The robot must be powered off and on again.

What the buzzes mean

  • 1 short = turned ON (auto-aim, gyro reset, backup spot). 2 short = turned OFF.
  • 1 long (both) = vacuum or dump started: the intake drops.
  • Operator steady = ready to fire. 3 long = hopper looks empty: go get balls.
  • Operator 1 / 2 / 3 short after Start = AUTO / SCORE / PASS.
  • Driver low hum = our hub is open. Pulses = hub swap in 5 s.
  • Driver fast pulses = pin (back off). Steady in the last 30 s = their tower, don't touch.
  • Both pulse early in teleop = no FMS game data: coach sets "Auto Winner".

Shooting with auto-aim (normal way)

  1. Auto-aim turns ON by itself when teleop starts (the turret follows our hub; the hood stays down until you shoot).
  2. Operator holds RT. The shooter spins up to the right speed for the distance and fires each ball the moment the shot is ready. The controller buzzes while it's ready.
  3. While scoring, the robot slows to shooting speed and locks its wheels when you let go of the sticks. Driver left stick click = full speed. If the hub is behind the turret, the robot turns itself a little (the turn stick always wins).

Shooting without auto-aim

  1. Aim with D-pad Left / Right (turret) and D-pad Up / Down or Y (hood).
  2. Hold RT: the shooter spins up and RT feeds once it is at speed (LT pre-spins).

Backup shots (if the camera is having trouble)

  1. Park on the spot: Midshot (between our hub and our climb), Left trench or Right trench (left / right as seen from our driver station).
  2. Operator holds A (Midshot), X (Left trench) or LB (Right trench) for half a second (buzz = set).
  3. Hold RT.
